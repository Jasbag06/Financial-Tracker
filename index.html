Home Screen & Back Tap

1. Add to Home Screen

Open Financial Tracker in Safari → tap the Share button (square with an arrow up) → "Add to Home Screen" → Add. The app icon appears on your home screen and opens without the address bar.

2. Set Up Back Tap

Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap → choose the Shortcut that opens Financial Tracker's URL. Double-tapping the back of your iPhone will then jump straight to the Add screen.
